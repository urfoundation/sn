This candidate adds `sn-mainnet validator-capacity-preview --request FILE --request-sha256 sha256:HASH` with the ordinary explicit durable-volume reference. It is an offline, unsigned resource proposal. It does not sign, publish configuration, provision keys, renew economic windows or authorize a restart.

The request pins the original production config, every configured operator's original signed ledger head, explicit roots and former-writer fences, finite physical inventory bounds, and separate future record/trail/byte/file forecasts. Snapshot leases remain held across the complete census. Missing original ledger checkpoints, unknown or absent roots, active writers, wrong heads and cancellation refuse the whole preview. The result retains the original authority bundle, complete physical and ledger reports, a proposed config, and the original economic approval body with only its full-config commitment changed. The existing independent approver must review and sign it separately.

`production_capacity_revision` is optional and omitted from historical JSON/hash paths. When present, the actual production loader authenticates an immediate-predecessor authority chain and a complete source census. Only explicitly enumerated aggregate history/storage/count limits can grow. Per-record/page/manifest/transport bounds, active provider/operator/participant counts, identities, policies and custody paths remain exact. Each new aggregate allowance must cover retained plus independently forecast future use with a factor of two; aggregate history must also cover the sum of all future source storage. Actual chunk/page and replay geometry remain bounded. The runtime checks the original signed prefix in each real opened ledger before publishing workers, preserving later completed progress.

This is a candidate implementation, not qualified behavior or a capacity approval. Its deterministic controls target actual public preview, signed config loading, unchanged old sidecar interpretation, exact retained prefix, incomplete/overflowing forecasts, missing custody, lease contention, cancellation and short output. Behavioral execution belongs to the separate Sol qualification. The fixed bootstrap member census and other roles' retention profiles remain separate bounds; 2,048 accounting slots do not imply 2,048 active, funded, resident or monitored members. Current source/module composition and the other PH/MG launch gates remain open.
