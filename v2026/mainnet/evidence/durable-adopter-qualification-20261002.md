## Recovery and service preflight

Unavailable/busy admission before mutation is retryable without poisoning the
owner or consuming a signed attempt. Publication uncertainty is distinct from
identity loss: dependent effects stop, the owner joins, and a fresh opener
reconciles the exact retained checkpoint. Restoring a path does not revive an
owner that already observed identity loss.

Root service recovery is an explicit, separately signed policy and command
(`prepare-recovery` / `run-recovery`). It binds the exact original runtime,
service state, volume declaration, validity window and finite allowance of at
most four opens in 24 hours with at least 60 seconds between attempts. Counter
admission pressure waits without consuming an open; uncertain reservation
starts no runtime. It preserves original native bytes and allowances. Omitted
recovery fields and hashes remain byte-identical; the old run command never
selects recovery, and the signed `Restart=no` unit is not rewritten. New
install/start effects require an explicitly reapproved storage-bearing unit.
Continuous production renewal and unattended
recovery beyond this bounded window remain open.

New unit approvals bind the exact storage reference. Install/start checks the
actual runtime's coordinator, operator, scratch and retained-input directories,
plus the separately approved operational directory. The production root
preflight executes the pinned inspector as the signed service UID/GID, reads a
bounded report and joins the process before any manager effect. Ordinary root
admission is not given a UID waiver. The inspector has no writable-owner API.
