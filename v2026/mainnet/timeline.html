SN25 mainnet launch timeline

From the version 1 launch to the first version 2 claim, by payout epoch. These are UR settlement epochs: epoch 0 lasted one day, and each later epoch lasts 7 days (50,400 blocks). They are not the chain's 72-minute tempo. All times are UTC, and a time marked ≈ is projected from its block number.

Note: the version 1 claim will not include all miners

Version 1 pays only the providers the validator has measured, and that measurement is capped at a cohort of about 1,500 providers per epoch (the live census levelled off at 1,471), so most miners get no v1 share; version 2 pays by usage and removes that cap.
