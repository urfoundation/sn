The initial derived receipt renderer treated a two-line positive artifact hash
file as a scalar. It aborted before RESULT.json or any manifest/seal existed.
This affected only derived TSV rendering; raw compiler/body receipts, source
inputs, binaries and tests were not changed. The original partial TSVs are
preserved with `.attempt1` names. The corrected renderer extracts one named
hash per lane and emits a scalar package terminal state.
