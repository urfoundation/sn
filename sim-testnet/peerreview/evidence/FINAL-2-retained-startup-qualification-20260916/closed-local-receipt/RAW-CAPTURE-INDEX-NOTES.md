This index binds closed local raw qualification files by source path and hash.
It includes the four original non-admitted compiler receipts, the scoped
literal-output cleanup inventory, and all corrected compiler/body receipts.
Compiled `.test` binaries are intentionally excluded from this raw-file index
because `BINARY-REPRODUCTION.tsv` records their hashes and raw capture
directories retain their paths; no binary is copied into the receipt.
